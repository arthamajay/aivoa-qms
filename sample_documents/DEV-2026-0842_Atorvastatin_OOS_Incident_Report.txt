1. INCIDENT SUMMARY & PROBLEM STATEMENT
During QC release HPLC testing (Method ATM-HPLC-019) of Atorvastatin Calcium API Batch ATOR-2610-044 isolated from ANFD-102 on 19-Oct-2026, an Out-of-Specification (OOS) result was obtained for oxidative degradation Impurity-B (0.38% w/w vs specification NMT 0.15% w/w) and amorphous content elevation (~14% by XRPD vs NMT 5%). Phase-I Laboratory Investigation confirmed no analytical error, column degradation, or integration fault; re-injection of duplicate preparations confirmed 0.37% and 0.39% w/w.

2. MANUFACTURING & SCADA / BMR EVIDENCE COLLECTED SO FAR
- [EV-01] SCADA Historian Log (GLR-204-TEMP-09): During Stage-IV exothermic neutralization and crystallization seeding on 18-Oct-2026 between 14:15 and 15:40, reactor internal temperature held at 64.8°C for 78 minutes (BMR limit: 58.0 - 61.0°C for max 30 minutes), followed by an erratic crash-cooling spike (-2.4°C/min) when chilled brine bypass was manually opened.
- [EV-02] Maintenance & Metrology Log (PM-2026-881): Proportional glycol/brine control valve PCV-204B on GLR-204 jacket exhibited actuator hysteresis and sticky stem behavior at 42% opening. Last elastomer diaphragm replacement was overdue by 45 days due to deferred shutdown.
- [EV-03] Gas Utility Header Log (N2-HDR-B): Nitrogen blanket pressure on GLR-204 dropped to 0.08 bar for 52 minutes during reactant charging because manifold regulator PRV-112 was shared with adjacent reactor GLR-205 during pressure purge, allowing ambient oxygen/moisture ingress at elevated temperature (64.8°C).
- [EV-04] Raw Material COA (Vendor Lot RM-2026-992): Recovered Methanol solvent ratio used in crystallization had moisture content 0.28% w/w (Spec: NMT 0.30% w/w — borderline high, up from historical mean 0.09% w/w).
- [EV-05] Electronic Batch Manufacturing Record (eBMR-ATOR-044): Step 14.3 lacks an automated SCADA interlock preventing reactant charge when N2 blanket pressure < 0.35 bar; operator logged manual pressure gauge reading only at start of shift (12:00) and did not notice the transient drop at 14:20.

3. IMMEDIATE CONTAINMENT ACTIONS TAKEN
- Placed Batch ATOR-2610-044 (450 kg) under QA Quarantine Hold in Warehouse Zone Q-03.
- Tagged Reactor GLR-204 "OUT OF SERVICE — ENGINEERING HOLD" pending valve PCV-204B teardown.
- Initiated look-back review of previous 3 campaign batches (ATOR-2610-041, -042, -043).
